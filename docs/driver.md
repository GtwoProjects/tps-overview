# Using the Driver

The Driver is a small program you install on your Windows machine. It runs in the background and handles the parts of a job that need to happen on your computer.

## Where to download it

We send you a download link in your welcome email. The file is called something like `TPS-Driver-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.

If you've lost the link, ask whoever set up your account.

## Installing on Windows

1. Double-click the installer.
2. Click through the prompts. The defaults are fine.
3. When it finishes, the Driver starts on its own. You'll see a small icon in your system tray (bottom-right corner of the screen).

That's the install. You don't need to do anything else for the Driver itself — it talks to the Dashboard automatically.

## Confirming it's connected

Right-click the tray icon. The menu shows the Driver's status.

"Connected" means it's talking to the Dashboard. "Disconnected" usually means a network issue or that you're not logged in to the Dashboard yet.

## What if it won't start?

A few things to try first:

- Reboot. Sounds dumb, often works.
- Right-click the installer and pick "Run as administrator", then reinstall.
- Check that your firewall isn't blocking it.

If none of that works, [file a bug](../../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include what version you installed and what your computer was telling you when it wouldn't start.
