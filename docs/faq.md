# FAQ

Short answers to things people ask a lot. If your question isn't here, [file an issue](../../../issues) and we'll add it.

## I forgot my password.

Use the "Forgot password" link on the Dashboard login screen. If that doesn't work, contact whoever gave you your account.

## The Driver tray icon is gone.

It probably crashed. Open the Start menu, search for "TPS Driver", and start it again. If it crashes again, [file a bug](../../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).

## A job is stuck on "Running" and isn't moving.

Wait a couple of minutes — some jobs take longer than others. If it's still stuck after that, [file a bug](../../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and include the job's ID (you can copy it from the Dashboard).

## I want a feature that's not in the product yet.

[Tell us about it.](../../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) Include why it would help — that's the part we use to decide what to work on first.
