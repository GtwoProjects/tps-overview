# Getting started

You're testing TPS. Here's what that means and how to do your first session.

## What TPS is

TPS turns content into finished videos through a few automated steps. As a tester, you'll touch two parts of it:

- The **Dashboard** — a website where you set up jobs and see results.
- The **Driver** — a small program that runs on your computer and does part of the work locally.

The Dashboard tells the Driver what to do. The Driver does the work and reports back. You watch it happen.

## Your first session

1. **Install the Driver.** The [Driver guide](driver.md) walks you through it. About two minutes on Windows.
2. **Log in to the Dashboard** at the URL we sent you. The [Dashboard guide](dashboard.md) shows you what to expect.
3. **Start a small job.** Pick the smallest option you see — you're checking that everything works end to end before you do anything bigger.
4. **Look at the result.** If it looks off, [tell us](../../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).

That's the whole first session. From here, work through whichever guide is relevant to what you're doing.
